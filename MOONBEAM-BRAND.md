# Moonbeam by NightRider — pilot brand guide

**Tagline:** Cut through the noise. See what it's worth.

**Creative direction:** A sliver of moonlight cuts through dark clouds and illuminates
what is hidden. The crescent belongs to NightRider's moon family, with a warm
orange-to-green gradient. Avoid cartoon spotlights, animal mascots, and excessive
visual clutter. The beam should be narrow and deliberate.

**Purpose:** Free read-only offer intelligence for independent drivers. Show
payout, pickup and trip mileage, driving time, and value in an understandable
way. Never accept, decline, tap, or decide for a driver.

**Brand architecture:** NightRider is the transportation ecosystem; Vela is
the navigation engine; Moonbeam is the driver-facing offer transparency tool.
Moonbeam is a gift to drivers, not a subscription funnel.

**Dark palette:** midnight #0B1625, slate #18283A, deeper surface #233B50,
moon amber #F4B167, sea-glass #74D7A4, accent mint #8EE6BE,
legible text #FFFFFF and #BED2DA. The sunny palette is cool mist, navy
text, and muted green.

**Public line:** Cut through the noise. See what it's worth.
**About easter egg:** Made to illuminate the bullshit.

**Security/upgrade identity remains unchanged:** Android package
`app.rideofferreader.driver`, permanent signing key, GitHub signed-release tag
prefix `ror-v`, and release asset `Ride-Offer-Reader.apk`. Never change these
just to rename the product. This preserves over-the-air updates and data.

**Launch caveat:** The name Moonbeam is not yet trademark-cleared. This is a
pilot rebrand. Obtain appropriate clearance before mass public promotion.
